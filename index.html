Pet Tips & Trivia

Did You Know? 💡

Expert tips and fun facts to help you be the best pet parent possible.

🐶
Did You Know?

Dogs have a sense of smell 40× stronger than humans — choose unscented cleaning products near their sleeping area to avoid overwhelming their senses.

🐱
Did You Know?

Cats need fresh water daily — consider a pet water fountain to encourage hydration and support long-term kidney health.

🐰
Did You Know?

Rabbits are social animals and do best in bonded pairs — they can become lonely and depressed if kept alone for extended periods.

🐟
Did You Know?

Fish can recognise their owners' faces — enrich their tanks with plants and hiding spots for a happy, stimulated, and stress-free fish.

🐦
Did You Know?

Birds need mental stimulation daily — rotate their toys every week to prevent boredom, reduce stress, and keep them mentally sharp.

🐹
Did You Know?

Guinea pigs cannot produce their own Vitamin C — supplement their diet with fresh bell peppers or leafy greens daily to prevent scurvy.

🦎
Did You Know?

Reptiles are cold-blooded and cannot regulate their own body temperature — a proper heat gradient in their enclosure is essential for digestion and immunity.

🐾
Did You Know?

Pets with consistent routines and enrichment live longer, healthier lives — interactive play reduces stress hormones and supports overall wellbeing.
